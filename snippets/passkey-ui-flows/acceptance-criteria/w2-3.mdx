## Acceptance criteria



| Benchmark as | Expected adoption impact |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy | High |

<Note>
    The relying party must define whether a passkey replaces the existing
    multi-step sign-in or acts as one factor within it. The acceptance criteria
    preserve either policy; they do not assume a specific MFA method.
</Note>

| ID | Level | Acceptance criterion | How to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **W2.3-AC01** | Core | The creation offer appears only after the account's complete existing MFA policy succeeds. | Test successful MFA, failed and abandoned additional-factor challenges, an expired MFA session, and any configured remembered-device path. The offer appears only after the policy reports complete success. |
| **W2.3-AC02** | Core | The authentication policy states whether the passkey replaces the existing multi-factor sign-in or acts as one factor within it. | Inspect the configured policy and its next-sign-in tests. Confirm that one supported role is defined for each included account group. |
| **W2.3-AC03** | Core | After the account's MFA sign-in completes, show a manual creation nudge that explains how a passkey improves the next sign-in, unless a passkey was already created by the automatic ceremony in W2.3-AC05. In the manual flow, the creation ceremony opens only after the user clicks Continue. | Complete MFA. Confirm that the manual nudge explains the future sign-in benefit and that the manual flow opens one creation ceremony only after Continue is clicked. One automatic ceremony under W2.3-AC05 does not fail this criterion. |
| **W2.3-AC04** | Core | When the passkey substitutes the existing multi-factor journey, registration and later authentication require and verify local user verification. | Register and authenticate with UV present, then repeat with UV absent or not requested. Only the UV-enforced path satisfies the substitution policy. |
| **W2.3-AC05** | Quality | On suitable clients, open one passkey-creation ceremony automatically after MFA completes. If it is cancelled or fails, show the manual creation nudge without an application error and wait for the user to click Continue before retrying. | Confirm that one ceremony opens automatically on a suitable client after MFA. Cancel or fail it, then confirm that the manual nudge appears without an error and no ceremony reopens until Continue is clicked. |
