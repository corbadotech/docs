## Acceptance criteria


| Benchmark as | Expected adoption impact |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy | High |

This flow covers the first-passkey nudge after a successful non-passkey sign-in to a single-factor account.


| ID | Level | Acceptance criterion | How to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **N2.1-AC01** | Core | Show this flow only after a successful non-passkey sign-in to a single-factor account on a client that can create a passkey, when the account has no registered passkey. | Test successful, failed and abandoned conventional sign-ins, a passkey sign-in, an MFA account, an account with a passkey and an unsupported client. Only the eligible single-factor account enters this flow. |
| **N2.1-AC02** | Core | After the eligible sign-in, show a manual creation nudge that explains how a passkey improves the next sign-in. The user can continue to creation or continue to the app without a passkey. | Complete an eligible sign-in. Confirm that the nudge explains the future sign-in benefit, Continue opens one creation ceremony, and skipping continues without creating a passkey. |
| **N2.1-AC03** | Core | The created passkey is stored for the authenticated account and can complete a later passkey sign-in to that same account. | Complete creation, sign out and use the new passkey to sign in again. The assertion resolves to the same account. |
| **N2.1-AC04** | Quality | On suitable clients, open one creation ceremony automatically after the eligible sign-in. If it is cancelled or fails before the configured attempt limit is reached, show the manual nudge without an application error and wait for the user to choose another attempt. Once the limit is reached, continue silently without another ceremony, nudge or error. | Cancel or fail the automatic attempt below the limit and confirm that the manual nudge appears without an error or immediate reopen. Reach the limit and confirm that the signed-in journey continues without another ceremony, nudge or error. |
