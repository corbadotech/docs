Corbado Observe data is available in the [Corbado Management Console](https://app.corbado.com). After your account has been invited and your organization's access has been configured, sign in there to view dashboards, analyze authentication journeys and investigate individual flows.

## Enterprise single sign-on

For enterprise customers, the Management Console supports single sign-on (SSO) through **SAML 2.0**. This allows your organization to authenticate console users with its existing identity provider (IdP), including **Okta**, **Microsoft Entra ID** or another SAML 2.0-compatible IdP.

If your organization uses on-premises Microsoft Active Directory, it must expose a SAML 2.0 identity provider, for example through **Active Directory Federation Services (AD FS)**. Active Directory Domain Services alone is not a SAML identity provider.

<Info>
  Contact your Corbado representative to configure enterprise SSO for your organization and invite the administrators who need access.
</Info>
